# Data Sources & Citations

## GIS & Planetary Visualization Tools
- **JMARS (Java Mission-planning and Analysis for Remote Sensing)**: Arizona State University (ASU) Mars Space Flight Facility (`https://jmars.asu.edu/`). Used to extract planetary/terrestrial Region of Interest (ROI) elevation meshes (`.stl`) and co-registered surface imagery/basemap layers (`.png`).

## Synthetic Verification Assets
- **`data/processed/test_site/`**: Procedurally generated synthetic crater-and-ridge binary STL mesh (`terrain.stl`, 2,048 triangles, Z-up meters) and 8x8 RGB checkerboard texture (`checkerboard.png`, 512x512 px) generated by `pipeline/validate_uv_test_site.py` to verify `DemExtent` planar UV alignment (`NW = (0, 0)`, `SE = (1, 1)`).

## Planetary & Terrestrial Mission Datasets (To be populated with exact ROI product IDs)
- **Moon**: Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) / Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) DEM and basemap products accessed via JMARS.
