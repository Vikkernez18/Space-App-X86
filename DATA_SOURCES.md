# Data Sources & Citations

## GIS & Planetary Visualization Tools
- **JMARS (Java Mission-planning and Analysis for Remote Sensing)**: Arizona State University (ASU) Mars Space Flight Facility (`https://jmars.asu.edu/`). Used to extract planetary/terrestrial Region of Interest (ROI) elevation meshes (`.stl`), digital elevation models (`.tif`/`.tiff`), and co-registered surface imagery/basemap layers (`.png`).

## Mathematical, Differential-Geometric & Topological Data Analysis (TDA) Methods
- **Slope Gradient $|\nabla z|$**: Computed via Horn's (1981) 3×3 finite-difference stencil over meter-projected DEM grids (`crates/core/src/terrain_analysis.rs` and `pipeline/score_analogs.py`).
- **Terrain Ruggedness Index (TRI)**: Riley, S. J., DeGloria, S. D., & Elliot, R. (1999). *A terrain ruggedness index that quantifies topographic heterogeneity*. Intermountain Journal of Sciences, 5(1–4), 23–27.
- **Hypsometric Integral ($HI$)**: Strahler, A. N. (1952). *Hypsometric (area-altitude) analysis of erosional topography*. Geological Society of America Bulletin, 63(11), 1117–1142.
- **0D Persistent Homology ($\beta_0$) & Elder Rule**: Edelsbrunner, H., & Harer, J. (2010). *Computational Topology: An Introduction*. American Mathematical Society. Sublevel and superlevel filtrations on the 2-manifold elevation grid extract persistent craters/basins and peaks/domes above a scale-invariant prominence threshold ($5\%$ of $\Delta z$).
- **Simplicial Euler–Poincaré Characteristic ($\chi = V - E + F$)**: Classical combinatorial topology invariant verified on every welded triangle mesh (`<= 50,000` triangles).

## Synthetic Verification Assets
- **`data/processed/test_site/`**: Procedurally generated synthetic crater-and-ridge binary STL mesh (`terrain.stl`, 8,192 triangles, Z-up meters) and 8x8 RGB checkerboard texture (`checkerboard.png`, 512x512 px) generated by `pipeline/validate_uv_test_site.py` to verify `DemExtent` planar UV alignment (`NW = (0, 0)`, `SE = (1, 1)`).
